Project Overview

Design a modern, professional, AI-powered legal web application for South Africa called LLAW (South African Legal Law WebApp).

The purpose of the application is to make South African laws, constitutional rights, contracts, government services, and legal information easy to understand for ordinary citizens.

This is NOT a law firm's website.

It is an AI-powered legal knowledge platform focused on legal education, document analysis, multilingual communication, accessibility, and government guidance.
